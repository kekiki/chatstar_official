Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chatstar ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects your personal information when you use our website and mobile application (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

The Service is developed and operated by an individual developer under the developer name "Chatstar". If you have any privacy question or request, you can contact us at chatstar8719@gmail.com. This policy, together with our Registration Agreement and Child Safety Policy, governs your use of the Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide: account details (phone number or email address, nickname, avatar, profile description, date of birth or age declaration), the content you post (photos, videos, comments), and the messages you send to other users.
  • Photos, videos, and audio: profile media you upload, content you publish, and your camera and microphone streams during live video or voice sessions.
  • Information collected automatically: device identifiers (such as advertising ID and app-scoped identifiers), device model, operating system, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, log data, crash diagnostics, and usage analytics (for example, features used, session duration, and interactions).
  • App activity: information about your activity in the Service, such as accounts you follow or block, content you report, and live sessions you join.
  • Information from third parties: limited account information (such as your email address and profile picture) when you choose to sign in with a third-party service, and billing confirmation from the app store when you make a purchase. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

We do not collect your precise GPS location. Location-based features, if any, rely only on coarse, IP-derived location.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To connect you with other users and deliver live video and voice sessions.
  • To verify your age for features with age restrictions, including live matching.
  • To detect, prevent, and address abuse, fraud, policy violations, and harm to our users, including through content moderation.
  • To improve, personalize, and optimize the Service, including by measuring feature performance and diagnosing crashes.
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, safety notices, and service changes.
  • To comply with our legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

4. Live Session Moderation

To keep the community safe, live video and voice sessions are subject to safety moderation, which combines automated detection (including audio, video, and text signals) with human review. When a session is flagged by automated systems or reported by a user, we may capture and review a portion of that session. Moderation records are retained only for as long as necessary for safety investigations and legal compliance, as described in Section 6.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only with:

  • Service providers: vendors who help us operate the Service (for example, hosting, content delivery, analytics, crash reporting, communications, and trust-and-safety moderation vendors), under strict confidentiality and data-protection obligations limiting their use of your data to providing services to us.
  • Authorities and hotlines: law enforcement, child-safety hotlines (such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children), and other authorities where required by law or to protect the safety of our users, including reporting child sexual exploitation and abuse as required by applicable law.
  • Other users: only to the extent you choose to share content with them, or where necessary to operate a feature you initiated (for example, delivering a call request).
  • Business transfers: a successor in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account information is retained while your account is active. If you request account deletion, we delete your account profile and personal details within 30 days of your request.
  • User content (posts, media, and messages) is retained while your account is active and is deleted with your account, except where we must preserve specific content for safety investigations or legal obligations.
  • Safety and moderation records (including records related to reports, violations, and account enforcement) may be retained for up to 24 months to detect repeat offenders and handle appeals.
  • Logs and diagnostics are retained for up to 12 months.
  • Residual copies in encrypted backups may persist for up to 90 days after deletion.

Account deletion: you can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings > Account > Delete Account, or by contacting us. Deleting your account removes your profile and personal information from the Service as described above.

7. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest for sensitive data, access controls, and organizational measures to protect your data. Access to personal information is limited to personnel and vendors who need it to perform their functions. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal. You can exercise these rights through in-app settings or by contacting us at chatstar8719@gmail.com. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

You can also control certain choices in the app, such as your profile visibility, who can contact you, notification preferences, and whether you receive personalized recommendations. If you are a resident of the EEA, UK, or California, additional statutory rights may apply, including the California right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — and because we do not sell or share your personal information as defined by that law, no further opt-out is required.

9. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions only when needed for the corresponding features: camera and microphone (to join live video or voice sessions; a black screen or mute is used until you enable them), notifications (to deliver calls and safety notices), and photo/media access (to upload profile pictures and content you choose to share). You may decline any permission, and the related features will be unavailable but the rest of the Service will keep working.

10. Children's and Teens' Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or a higher age where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. Random live video matching is restricted to users aged 18 and above. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under the minimum age, we promptly delete it. Accounts belonging to ineligible children are removed as soon as we discover them.

For users between the minimum age and 17, the Service applies additional privacy-protective defaults, including limited discoverability and contact restrictions, as described in our Child Safety Policy.

11. International Data Transfers

We operate globally, and your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we apply appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and additional measures to protect your data.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the Service (for example, an in-app notice) or by other means before the change takes effect, and we will update the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, contact us at chatstar8719@gmail.com. Privacy questions from users in the EEA, UK, or other jurisdictions with statutory response requirements are handled by our privacy team and answered within the statutory deadlines.
