Child Safety Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The safety of young people is a top priority at Chatstar. This Child Safety Policy explains the measures we take to protect minors who use our platform, our zero tolerance for child abuse, and how everyone can help keep our community safe. It forms part of, and should be read together with, our Registration Agreement and Privacy Policy.

1. Our Commitment

We are committed to building a platform where people can connect and share safely. We invest in product safeguards, around-the-clock moderation, and cooperation with authorities and safety organizations to prevent exploitation and abuse. We follow a safety-by-design approach: safety is considered at every stage of product development, not added afterwards.

2. Age Requirements and Age Assurance

  • Users must be at least 13 years old (or the equivalent minimum age in their jurisdiction) to hold an account.
  • Live one-on-one video matching is restricted to users aged 18 and above; age assurance is applied before this feature is enabled.
  • We use a combination of age declarations, age signals at registration, and account-behavior checks to identify potentially underage users on age-restricted features.
  • Accounts belonging to children under the minimum age are removed as soon as we become aware of them, and their data is promptly deleted as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Users who misrepresent their age to access age-restricted features are removed, and attempts to re-register are blocked.

3. Safety by Design

  • Privacy-protective defaults for accounts of users under 18, including limited discoverability and restrictions on who can contact them.
  • Reporting and blocking tools available in every chat, profile, and live session.
  • 24/7 content moderation combining automated detection (image, video, audio, and text signals) with human review, including real-time intervention during live sessions.
  • No precise location sharing, and no public contact details, for users under 18.
  • Safety notices and reminders at the moments they matter most, for example when a minor receives a message from a newly created account or an adult they do not know.
  • Protection against unwanted contact, including filtering of friend requests and messages for teen accounts.

4. Zero Tolerance for Abuse

We have zero tolerance for child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, and any behavior that endangers minors. When we detect such activity, we:

  • permanently ban the offending accounts and block attempts to return;
  • preserve evidence in accordance with applicable law;
  • report incidents to the relevant authorities and hotlines, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline where applicable, and to hotlines in other regions where required;
  • support removal of images and videos of abuse, including by referring affected users to tools such as NCMEC's Take It Down.

5. How to Report and What Happens Next

Use the report button in any chat, profile, or live session, or contact our safety team directly. Reports from minors and their guardians are treated as urgent and prioritized for review.

  • We acknowledge urgent child-safety reports as quickly as possible and prioritize their review ahead of other content.
  • Actions we may take include removing content, ending live sessions, suspending or permanently banning accounts, and involving authorities where necessary.
  • Reporters receive feedback on the outcome where appropriate.
  • Anyone affected by an enforcement decision may appeal by responding to the in-app notice or contacting us.

6. Support for Young Users and Guardians

If a young person on Chatstar is experiencing harm or feeling unsafe, they should end the session, use the report and block tools, and tell a trusted adult. If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first, then report to us so we can take urgent action. We provide clear in-app guidance for reporting and getting help.

7. Education and Resources

We provide in-app safety tips and prompts covering topics such as protecting personal information, recognizing grooming and unsafe requests, and how to report concerns. We also point users and guardians to trusted external resources, including:

  • NCMEC CyberTipline: report.cybertip.org
  • NCMEC Take It Down (help removing intimate images of minors): takeitdown.ncmec.org
  • ConnectSafely guides for parents and teens: connectsafely.org
  • Internet Matters: internetmatters.org

We partner with safety organizations and keep this guidance up to date.

8. Parental Guidance

We encourage parents and guardians to talk with teens about online safety, review privacy settings together, and make use of the parental control features provided by mobile operating systems, such as Google Play's parental controls and content rating settings. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's account should be removed or restricted, contact us and we will take action.

9. Working with Experts and Authorities

We cooperate with law enforcement agencies, child-safety hotlines, and industry safety initiatives worldwide, and we respond to valid legal requests in accordance with applicable law and our Privacy Policy.

10. Contact Us
