Child Safety Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The safety of young people is a top priority at Chatstar. This Child Safety Policy explains the measures we take to protect minors who use our platform and how everyone can help keep our community safe.

1. Our Commitment

We are committed to building a platform where young people can connect and share safely. We invest in product safeguards, moderation, and cooperation with authorities and safety organizations to prevent exploitation and abuse.

2. Age Requirements

  • Users must be at least 13 years old (or the equivalent minimum age in their jurisdiction) to hold an account.
  • Live one-on-one video matching is restricted to users aged 18 and above.
  • Accounts belonging to children under the minimum age are removed as soon as we become aware of them.

3. Safety by Design

  • Privacy-protective defaults for minor accounts, including limited discoverability.
  • Reporting and blocking tools available in every chat and live session.
  • 24/7 content moderation combining automated detection with human review.
  • Suspicious behavior detection on live streams, including real-time intervention.
  • No precise location sharing or public contact details for minors.

4. Zero Tolerance for Abuse

We have zero tolerance for child sexual exploitation and abuse, grooming, sextortion, and any behavior that endangers minors. We permanently ban offenders, preserve evidence, and report incidents to the relevant authorities and hotlines, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.

5. Parental Guidance

We encourage parents and guardians to talk with teens about online safety, review privacy settings together, and make use of the parental control features provided by mobile operating systems. If you believe your child is at risk, contact us immediately and we will take urgent action.

6. Education and Resources

We provide in-app safety tips and prompts covering topics such as protecting personal information, recognizing unsafe requests, and how to report concerns. We also partner with safety organizations to keep our guidance up to date.

7. How to Report

Use the report button in any chat or profile, or contact our safety team directly. Reports from minors and their guardians are treated as urgent and prioritized for review. We will acknowledge reports promptly and take appropriate action, including removing content, suspending accounts, and involving authorities where necessary.

8. Contact Us

If you have concerns about the safety of a young person on Chatstar, contact our safety team at safety@chatstar.app.
